First visit? Questions, answered.

What services does Hasku & Tails offer?

Consultation, an in-house diagnostic center for blood tests, X-ray, surgery and a pharmacy, all in one clinic. Sonography is opening soon.

Where is the clinic located?

Survey no 311/3, in front of Japalouppe Equestrian Centre, Old Mumbai-Pune Hwy, Somatne Phata, Talegaon Dabhade, Maharashtra 410506. Tap Plan your visit for directions on Google Maps.

How do I book an appointment?

Tap Book a visit, add your pet's details and preferred time, and send it to us on WhatsApp. You can also call +91 91753 61146.

Can I find out the cost before I visit?

Yes. Tap Check price on any service and message us on WhatsApp. Our team will explain the cost for your pet's needs.

Are tests and X-rays done at the clinic?

Yes. Blood tests are handled at our in-house diagnostic center and X-ray is done on site, so your vet can review results without sending you elsewhere.

What should I bring to a first visit?

Any previous medical records or vaccination card, a note of your pet's symptoms and when they started, and any current medicines. A carrier or towel helps nervous pets.

When will sonography start?

Soon. Message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you the day it opens.
