Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Hasku & Tails is operated by Immerce Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you request an appointment, we collect your name, mobile number, your pet's type and name, the service you want, and your preferred date and time if you give one. If you message us on WhatsApp or call, we also receive your number and what you tell us.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your request and confirm your appointment.
  • To share prices and service information you ask for.
  • To contact you about that request. We will not use your details to send unrelated promotions without asking you first.
  • To improve our website and our advertising, using anonymous usage data.

Your consent

We collect this data only with your consent, which you give by ticking the box on our form or by messaging us. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

Who sees your data

  • Our clinic team handling enquiries and appointments.
  • Service providers we use to run the business, such as WhatsApp (Meta), Google (Sheets, email, Maps, and advertising or analytics tools if enabled), and our website host (GitHub).
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

Cookies and advertising

This website may use cookies and similar tools, including Google Ads and Google Analytics, to measure visits and show relevant ads. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this site. You can manage ad personalisation in your Google account settings or block cookies in your browser.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond to you and manage your appointments, and as required by law. After that we delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your details earlier.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, erase it, or stop using it, and you can withdraw your consent or nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. Write to us at the contact below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is for pet owners and is not meant for anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data. No online system is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive information such as financial details through the form.
